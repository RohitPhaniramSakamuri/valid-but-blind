# Adversarial novelty review: adaptation-induced monitoring blind spots

Review and search date: **2 November 2026, Asia/Kolkata**. Requested period: 2017–today, with earlier foundations. This review tries to find predecessors, including work outside TTA. No experiments, model runs, implementation tests, or paid compute were performed. Python was used only for retrieval and record management through `uv run --no-project`.

## 1. What the local record actually establishes

The [Experiment 004 record](../../experiments/004-monitor-blind-spots.md) reports a frozen replication rule passing on CIFAR-100-C using the project's monitor port: Tent missed **147/166** strict violating streams; source missed **44/110**. The same six model/checkpoint arms and paired external draws were used. These are empirical stream counts, not population risks or 276 independent samples. Each arm has its own violating subset. Model/image reuse, replacement sampling, selected corruption families, one Tent configuration, and the ported implementation limit the finding. A strict violation uses final error at least r₀+0.15, while the released-form alarm threshold is r₀+0.10. The experiment excludes streams by a last-quarter error cutoff; that is an operational collapse filter, not a proof that representations or predictions cannot collapse. [Local protocol/results](../../experiments/004-monitor-blind-spots.md#tent-versus-source-replication-protocol-frozen-1-november-2026-before-any-stream-ran); [strict-event definition](../../diagnostics/2026-11-01-exp004-replicate/analyze_replicate.py).

The record's paired subset is stronger evidence than the pooled percentages alone: on 97 draws where both arms violate, Tent alone was missed on 49 and source alone on 2. Excess-error bands also favor greater Tent misses. These controls reduce, but do not eliminate, differences in detectability; the draws share models, images, and corruption conditions. The six-model confidence prediction previously **failed**: model-average confidence did not explain which models were missed. That negative result must remain alongside C3's proposed mechanism. [Local results and failed confidence prediction](../../experiments/004-monitor-blind-spots.md).

The final section of that record freezes a factorial protocol and authors'-pipeline check and ends **“Result: pending.”** Earlier patched author-code runs used uniform/by-class streams and different calibration/streams; they were not an exact Dirichlet or port comparison. Existing output filenames are not substituted for a completed authoritative analysis. This review did not run those analyses or infer their verdict. Thus C2 is assessed as a literature claim and an unconfirmed local proposition. ## 2. Claim-by-claim evidence

### C1: exact monitor, skew regime, and adapted/frozen miss comparison

**Closest direct predecessor.** Schirmer §5.4/Fig. 5 already shows uncertainty-based lower bounds failing to trigger under severe ImageNet-C corruption for T3A/STAD, and improves the proxy using prototype distance. Appendix A.5/Fig. 10 instead shows successful earlier detection on class-ordered, binary Yearbook streams. The latter does not establish power under persistent many-class Dirichlet priors. Neither inspected experiment gives C1's Tent-versus-frozen miss-rate comparison. Main paper §§5.1–5.4, Appendix A, and figure panels were inspected; Fig. 5 was also rendered locally. Official-PDF appendix pages differ from arXiv v2: A.2 is p24 versus p17; A.5 is p28 versus p21. [Primary paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/746960ad49ddb47248970a0e1404230c-Paper-Conference.pdf); [saved Fig. 5](evidence/root/Schirmer-Fig5.png).

**Closest compound-stream predecessor.** The published Cygert study §5.2/Fig. 6 evaluates corrupted CIFAR-100 with Dirichlet class correlation, including δ=0.1, and shows proxy-based selection separating further from oracle selection under stronger correlation. Figs. 7–8 show objective-tied entropy selection favoring degraded models. However, this is selection quality for SAR/EATA/AdaContrast in that figure, not sequential risk alarms for Tent and source. A shared Dirichlet parameter does not identify the same sampling process: this project's global prior is held persistent with replacement sampling, while correlated/order-aware samplers can produce different local class exposure. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.5220/0014320100004084), §5.2/Figs. 6–8; [local stream construction](../../diagnostics/2026-10-31-exp004/run_monitor.py).

**Closest general monitoring predecessor.** Han Appendix L demonstrates adapted forecasting scores absorbing sustained shifts while a frozen residual signal remains detectable. Its revealed-outcome forecasting setting is a material difference from label-free confidence monitoring; it defeats a broad first-discovery story, not this exact classification result. PPRM Fig. 2a also reports Schirmer's URM failing to alarm in its test period, but with a static CIFAR-10 model and true-label-assisted alternative. [Han](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07038), Appendix L/Tables 48–51; [PPRM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/zhang26g.html), §5.1.2/Fig. 2a.

**Assessment.** Measuring this particular failure remains distinguishable from those experiments. It does not establish that the difference is important enough for a standalone paper. A reviewer can reasonably ask why Schirmer's own documented proxy failure plus existing skew/entropy failures did not already predict it. The response would require evidence that online source recalibration fails in a consequential, nondegenerate regime and an explanation more specific than “entropy reduces entropy.” This is our assessment, not a claim made by the cited authors.

### C2: fixed threshold and entropy versus BN

Fixing the proxy threshold addresses one confound; it does not alone isolate which entropy-induced representation, classifier, calibration, or decision-boundary change matters. The planned same-logit threshold comparisons and BN-only arm are appropriate controls, but their outcome remains unrecorded. A failed BN-only cross-model criterion would mean that sufficiency was not established by that design; it would not prove BN never contributes. [Pending local factorial protocol](../../experiments/004-monitor-blind-spots.md#mechanism-and-authors-pipeline-test-frozen-1-november-2026-before-any-stream-ran).

The most serious unresolved predecessor is Mohammad Reza Mahdiani, *Diagnosing and repairing the conformal coverage guarantee of test-time-adapted image classifiers*, SSRN7602585, posted 12 October 2026. Its primary indexed abstract claims entropy-manufactured confidence and a matched-accuracy BN-statistics-only control on CIFAR/ImageNet corruptions. The landing page and PDF returned 403. **Those claims are discovery leads, not established full-text findings in this review.** They directly block any assertion that the proposed BN control has never appeared. No label-prior experiment is inferred from that abstract. [Primary record](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7602585); [access evidence and scope](agents/adjacent_monitors/report.md).

Older over-certainty work, AETTA, and SICL constrain the broad cause, but do not supply the complete fixed-threshold/BN/Schirmer comparison in the inspected methods and experiments. The exact causal contrast remains both locally pending and literature-incomplete. [Amin–Kim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16168); [SICL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07390).

### C3: confidence, error, and suppressed alarms

AETTA §3.2/Fig. 2 is direct prior evidence that Tent's confidence can rise while accuracy falls. Its plotted confidence is an expectation over dropout predictions, not necessarily the port's exact maximum-softmax statistic; its separate estimator comparisons also evaluate SoftmaxScore. Amin–Kim analyzes over-certainty and regularizes it; SICL §4 studies progressive miscalibration and supplies a consistency-based correctness signal. Cygert explicitly documents the hazard of selecting with the objective being optimized. A recent intrusion-detection audit formalizes how confidence/ATC and agreement can be blind to confidently wrong or correlated collapse. These are materially closer than generic calibration citations. [AETTA](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lee_AETTA_Label-Free_Accuracy_Estimation_for_Test-Time_Adaptation_CVPR_2024_paper.html), §3.2/Fig. 2; [Amin–Kim](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16168), methods/experiments; [SICL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07390), §§4–6; [Ahmed et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1928858), §4.3/Propositions 1–2.

**Important distinction:** Schirmer's monitor does not merely average raw confidence or assume perfect probability calibration. It thresholds uncertainty with source labels, recalibrating after adaptation, and needs useful error separation. A monotone score transformation shared by calibration and test data can leave threshold exceedances unchanged. Therefore overconfidence by itself does **not** imply the observed alarm gap. The specific mechanism to establish is changed flagged-error/flagged-correct mass relative to source calibration. This distinction follows from the method, and prevents overclaiming either originality or causal proof. [Schirmer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08721), §§3.2–3.4, Appendix A.2.

CBPE illustrates the simpler special case: calibrated-confidence performance estimation is conditional on calibration. Increasing predicted confidence without accuracy improvement biases that estimate. It is a useful baseline and an analytical warning, not evidence that a separately calibrated sequential lower bound must fail in the same way. [Kivimäki et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10994-025-06970-3), §§3–6/Theorem 1/Table 2.

### C4: the general self-masking claim

The architecture-level idea is established across several settings: adaptive residual charts absorb process changes; adaptive conformal references normalize sustained shifts; a forecasting adapter can erase its own residual signal; learned activation monitors become stale after model updates. These concern different parts of the monitoring pipeline and must not be treated as interchangeable theorems. [Messaoud et al.](https://hdl.handle.net/10419/22573), §4 p6/§5.4 p13/Fig. 6; [Han](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07038), Appendix L; [Shaer et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/shaer26a.html), §3.1/§4; [Duan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15980), pp2–7.

Atarmla gives exact evidence-budget/blinding results for an adaptive scalar quantile threshold, with persistent steps and ramps. Its model is not a proof about every confidence-derived TTA monitor, and C2 deliberately removes the threshold adaptation it studies. Conversely, Hong's world-model drift paper does not specify online world-model updates in its tested protocol; citing it as experimental proof that weight adaptation causes masking would overstate the source. [Atarmla](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23254), §§2–7/Appendix A; [Hong](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08455), §§3–5/Appendices A–E.

A defensible reformulation is: *A monitor's power can deteriorate when adaptation changes the relationship between its score and correctness; validity and power need separate assessment.* Claiming all confidence monitors can be defeated under the same allowed shift class requires stating that class, information access, calibration regime, false-alarm requirement, and alternative to be detected. No such universal result was established here.

### C5: existing alternatives and their limits

Existing options include Schirmer's alternative proxy, AETTA dropout correction/recovery, TTALine agreement-based performance estimation/selection, SICL perturbation consistency, a separately trained harmful-shift error estimator, D3M/Detectron disagreement, source/live segmentation geometry, frozen-feature certificate gates, conformal model/data-shift correction, and external verifier scores. Their information and calibration budgets differ. “Label-free” here means **no target outcomes at runtime**; most require labeled source data, offline labeled development, extra training, or additional model passes. [AETTA](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lee_AETTA_Label-Free_Accuracy_Estimation_for_Test-Time_Adaptation_CVPR_2024_paper.html); [TTALine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04941), §5.2/Table 3/Fig. 3 and §7 (multiple candidates and ID data); [D3M](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.05047); [CUI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02998); [CASTER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00374); [prediction fragmentation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20700).

Several apparent solutions do not satisfy C5: Han requires revealed forecast outcomes; PPRM corrects synthetic labels with actual target labels; WATCH's main outcome-conformity channel needs outcomes, while its input-only channel detects shift rather than risk. The inspected Shaer/Liu conditional-conformal methods assume a fixed scoring function. Static conditional-shift certificates are not general continual-Tent guarantees. CASTER's reported poor transfer of its independent gate to Tent is a direct warning that independence alone does not suffice. See the extraction matrix below and the [adjacent-source report](agents/adjacent_monitors/report.md), with exact methods, experiments, and label requirements.

**Our interpretation:** a frozen input sentinel can miss purely learning-induced harm, while disagreement can miss shared confidently wrong predictions. Without restrictions, the same unlabeled observations can be paired with different labels and different true risks. An arbitrary-shift guarantee of both low false alarms and sensitive risk detection is therefore unavailable from those observations alone. This is the established identifiability problem, not a new impossibility claim. [Garg et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.04234), §3.1; [local source note](../../papers/Garg2022ATC.md).


**Late adversarial-search predecessors.** ZAEC already isolates confidence inflation on examples whose top-1 prediction and correctness stay fixed, then repairs confidence with a frozen-source entropy floor without changing those predictions. It studies VLM calibration, not this sequential alarm. StaR-TTA repairs confidence-estimator staleness after offline RL model updates without target correctness labels; it is not continual entropy TTA. PORTAL instead needs sparse target labels. These strengthen the existing-method objection to a generic C5 proposal. [ZAEC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05945), Table1/Props1–2/pp3–7; [StaR-TTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35500), §§3–5/AppendixA; [PORTAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37658), §4.6/AppendixX; [reading/version limits](agents/tta_causes/late-screen.md). ZAEC refers to appendices absent from its downloaded nine-page version; their contents were not assumed.

**The named Entropy Enigma predecessor** uses weighted prediction flips during adaptation to estimate initial/source-model dataset accuracy. Its flip-to-accuracy mapping uses labeled offline data; this is not an assumption-free current-model risk alarm. [Press etal.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05012), §§4.1–4.4; [explicit extraction](agents/tta_causes/report.md).

## 3. Why validity can coexist with blindness

This is an algebraic reading of the existing 0–1-loss monitor, **not a new theorem or experiment**. Let r be error probability, q the probability the proxy flags a point, FP the joint probability of flagged-and-correct, and FN the joint probability of unflagged-and-wrong. These are joint masses, not class-conditional FPR/FNR. At any time,

\[
q=r-\mathrm{FN}+\mathrm{FP},\qquad
B=q-\mathrm{FP}_0,\qquad
r-B=\mathrm{FN}-\mathrm{FP}+\mathrm{FP}_0.
\]

Use running averages for the time-varying terms. Schirmer's Assumption 1 makes the last quantity nonnegative, giving the population lower bound B; confidence-sequence penalties make the implemented lower bound more conservative. Large proxy misses FN can make the validity condition easier to satisfy while leaving the bound too low to cross the alarm threshold. Increasing stream length cannot remove a persistent **population proxy gap**, although it can reduce statistical uncertainty. [Schirmer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08721), Assumption 1/Proposition 1/Corollary 1/Appendix B.4.

For a constructed illustration, r=0.50, FN=0.35, FP=0.05, and FP₀=0.17 give q=0.20 and B=0.03. The lower bound is valid but cannot reveal a 0.30 risk threshold in its population limit. This is arithmetic, not a fitted explanation of these runs. Demonstrating that Tent systematically changes these masses relative to BN/source is the missing empirical causal work; average confidence or ECE alone is insufficient.

## 5. Reviewer objections

**1. “The guarantee controls false alarms, not detection power.” — Correct, and serious.** A non-alarm is not a safety certificate. Schirmer itself discusses conservative bounds and documents proxy failures. The study must be framed as a quantified limit of practical sensitivity, with valid assumptions and consequential excess risks, rather than a theorem violation. To add value it should distinguish proxy information loss from finite-sample conservatism and show consequences beyond the paper's existing §5.4. [Schirmer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08721), Appendix A.1/§5.4/§6; [Podkopaev–Ramdas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06177), §2.2.

**2. “This follows from known over-certainty.” — Strong against C3 and broad framing; incomplete against exact C1.** Over-certainty is documented. However, online source threshold recalibration can compensate for pure score scaling; it is not enough to observe higher average confidence. The useful residual question concerns source-to-target error-separation changes, their effect on the actual bound, and whether a practical remedy improves detection. [AETTA](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Lee_AETTA_Label-Free_Accuracy_Estimation_for_Test-Time_Adaptation_CVPR_2024_paper.html), §3.2; [Schirmer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.08721), §3.4/A.2.

**3. “Dirichlet skew is artificial or already known.” — Both aspects deserve attention.** Dirichlet class streams and combined corruption/prior shift are established evaluation settings; proposing them is not a contribution. A controlled synthetic stress case can still expose an important limit, but alpha=0.1 alone does not establish deployment prevalence or comparability across samplers. Report class support, prior concentration, temporal exposure, replacement/reuse, and compare a natural or externally motivated skew regime before generalizing. [Cygert](https://doi.org/10.5220/0014320100004084), §5.2/Fig. 6; [TTAB](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/zhao23d.html), §6/Appendix F; [Label Shift Adapter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08810), §4/Tables 1–2. TTAB's cited clean label-shift experiment must not be relabeled as a combined-corruption experiment.

**4. “Frozen and Tent have different errors or streams.” — Partly addressed, not settled.** External draws are paired in the recorded replication, so “different streams” is not an accurate blanket objection. But conditioning on each arm's own violations changes the compared populations; source calibration and event margins also vary by model. The shared-violation and excess-error reports strengthen the case, while repeated model/image use and correlated draws limit uncertainty statements. Fixed-threshold/same-logit comparisons and error-mass decomposition are more useful next than pooling more streams. These are our methodological judgments grounded in the [local replication record](../../experiments/004-monitor-blind-spots.md).

**5. “Current TTA already solves it.” — Not established, but Tent-only scope is a weakness.** SAR, DeYO, ROID, COME, imbalance-aware adaptations, and calibration modules directly target relevant failure sources. Better accuracy does not establish monitor sensitivity, and replacing Tent can change both which streams violate and the calibration signal. Current-method comparisons should measure excess risk, misses, and false alarms under the same access/budget, not transfer published accuracy rankings. Recent open-set and medical work still reports confident errors despite stronger methods. [COME](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10894), methods/experiments; [Li–Shelhamer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01973), §4.6/Tables 1,10; [Nielen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02339), §5.2/Appendix G. No literature result inspected proves these methods solve the exact monitor failure.

## 6. Recommendation and stopping criteria

**Defensible now:** a reproducible, carefully bounded observation that a supported confidence monitor can have substantially worse sensitivity on an adapted model than on its frozen counterpart under this persistent skew/corruption construction. State that this extends a documented proxy-failure family. Do not assert the entropy cause, general immunity/failure theorem, or a working fix. Pending implementation and factorial results are prerequisites for strengthening that statement.

Prioritize later work in this order, subject to a separate execution request:

1. Finish and document the already frozen author-pipeline/port and fixed-threshold/BN checks; preserve failed and negative outcomes. Resolve Mahdiani full text before pitching those controls as a contribution.
2. Explain the relevant proxy-error masses and source/target threshold behavior. Separate finite-sample delay from a population bound that stays below threshold.
3. Compare meaningful existing remedies at matched false alarms: prototype distance, AETTA/SICL/CUI, independent error/disagreement/geometry, and a frozen sentinel. Include the cost of source buffers, extra models, perturbations, and labeled development.
4. Test scope with a fresh dataset and current stable/imbalance-aware TTA methods. A natural skew setting is more informative than many near-identical CIFAR streams.

**Continue only if** the exact comparison survives implementation and fair-comparison checks and reveals something useful beyond known over-certainty. **Change direction** if it reduces to a trivial score-scale effect, disappears under a standard proxy or modern adaptation baseline, or the blocked close predecessor contains the same controlled result. **Stop the broad theory/“independent alarm” pitch now:** those principles have substantial prior art. A method paper needs a demonstrated improvement over that prior art; a diagnostic paper needs a strong, transferable measurement and explanation. These are judgments, not publication predictions.
