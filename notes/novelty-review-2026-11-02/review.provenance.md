## What the counts mean

The final ledgers contain **388 search/retrieval rows**, **2,818 distinct screened title/source records**, and **383 evidence files**. Most screens are mechanical official-title matches or index snippets; these numbers do not mean thousands of full-paper readings. Index pages, duplicate PDF/text formats, retrieval errors, figure images, and raw API responses are evidence files, not distinct papers. Per-query web total hits often were not exposed; logs say so rather than invent counts. Search totals from broad index queries are not relevant-paper counts.
