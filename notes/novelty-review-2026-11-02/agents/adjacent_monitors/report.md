## Findings that most threaten originality

1. **Self-masking by adaptation is explicitly documented in Han2026Exchangeability, with an independent frozen signal comparison.** Its Appendix L, pp45–49, Tables48–51, goes substantially beyond the abstract. The learner absorbs a sustained level shift within one 96-step forecasting horizon; its monitoring scores then fall below their pre-change mean, whereas the frozen backbone's residuals remain elevated. On the learner's scores the stride96/e-detector constructions seldom or never detect the change promptly (Table49); a frozen-residual e-detector detects27/45 magnitude 1 and44/45 magnitude 3 changes within480steps against0/45 clean twins (Table50). This is strong prior art for the general feedback/self-masking idea and independent sentinel architecture. **Boundary:** the monitor sees revealed forecast outcomes, not label-free classification confidence; it monitors a Kalman adapter, not Tent/Schirmer; guarantees are explicitly not established on these dependent score streams. The broad possibility is published; a universal statement about *every* confidence-derived monitor is not proved.

2. **The general mechanism predates TTA by decades in statistical process control.** Messaoud2004ResidualControl §4 p6 explains why parameters at time`t−1`, rather than`t`, are used to calculate residuals/control limits: using current estimates can mask changes. §5.4 p13 and Figure6 report that after an identified process change, residual Shewhart charts stop signalling as the shift is transferred into adaptive parameter estimates. This is explicitly called the masking/parameter-adaptation problem. It is a drilling time-series process, not confidence-based model-risk monitoring, but defeats claiming the abstract principle as new. Full methods, §5 experimental interpretation and appendix map were inspected; no mathematical proof was independently verified.

3. **CBPE already formalizes the dependency of label-free performance monitoring on trustworthy confidence.** Kivimaki2026CBPE §§3–4, Theorem1 and §6 condition guarantees on calibration (and confidence-consistency). §5.3/Table2 demonstrate performance-estimation errors growing with calibration deterioration on TableShift. NannyML's official documentation, “Assumptions and Limitations”, likewise requires calibrated probabilities and disclaims concept-shift and unseen-region reliability. For multiclass accuracy its estimator is the mean probability assigned to the predicted class; if an update raises that quantity without corresponding accuracy gains, overestimation follows directly. This last conditional statement is our algebraic inference, **not** their experiment with Tent. Their paper has no TTA or sequential anytime-valid alarm-power study.

4. **A very close October2026 preprint is inaccessible in full:** Mahdiani2026ConformalTTA, *Diagnosing and repairing the conformal coverage guarantee of test-time-adapted image classifiers*, SSRN7602585, posted12October2026,34pages. The indexed primary abstract explicitly claims endogenous confidence inflation, a BN-statistics-only matched-accuracy control, Tent/EATA/SAR/CoTTA on CIFAR10-C/CIFAR100-C/ImageNet-C, failure of clean-source recalibration, and predict-before-update repair. The primary landing page and direct PDF delivery both returned403. **Do not treat this as a full-text established collision.** It is a material unresolved predecessor for C2–C4; its abstract alone prevents declaring that those controls were never previously attempted. No label-skew experiment can be inferred from the abstract. The citation-tracing agent independently discovered it and owns the detailed version trail.

“Independent” must specify independence from the optimized statistic or frozen model state; it does **not** mean statistical independence of source/current prediction errors. A frozen sentinel can detect external shift while failing to distinguish harmful from beneficial shifts. Source/live disagreement does not identify which model is correct without assumptions. These are interpretations of the method definitions above.

## Additional adjacent screens

**Late audit additions:** `Duan2026MonitorStaleness` (arxiv 2606.15980v2, Evan Duan) was read in the methods, Tables 3–5, mechanism controls, repair section and limitations, pp 2–7. Frozen LLM activation probes lose AUROC and low-FPR recall after LoRA updates; paired unlabeled base/update activations realign the probe inputs. This supplies adjacent model-update/monitor-signal and label-free-repair prior art. The score is an activation probe, not class-confidence risk; there is no sequential Tent/Schirmer/class-skew experiment. Its AAAI 2027 copyright footer is not independently verified acceptance: cite it as a 2026 preprint.

`Ponduru2025TrustAdapt` was screened through RePEc's bibliographic abstract. It describes a frozen-source confidence/conformal/Hoeffding trigger, source anchoring, selective memory and rollback. **The publisher landing page and PDF both returned 403.** The index assigns volume 11(2), March 2025; that metadata was not cross-checked against the blocked paper. This is an unresolved C5 predecessor; no verdict here depends on assuming its complete method or experiments. The main SSRN blocker above and this source both limit completeness.

- Rabanser2019FailingLoudly: official NeurIPS2019 abstract and metadata only. Two-sample testing after frozen classifier dimensionality reduction already provides a label-free external shift sentinel. Not used as evidence of a specific TTA result.
- Stocco2022AdaptiveDrivingMonitor: primary PMC/Wiley §§3–4 and discussion excerpts. Online adaptation of an autoencoder monitor uses driving uncertainty/lateral deviation to select retraining data; discussion warns that coupling can teach away anomalous behaviour. No entropy-TTA classification comparison; screened as architecture precedent, not exact finding.
- Kim2025ClinicalMonitoring: primary PMC methods,results/figures,discussion excerpts and PubMed metadata. Feedback-aware adherence/sampling weighting corrects deployment-induced label modification and harmful retrains; requires outcomes/treatment assumptions, not label-free TTA.
- Tang2026REFIT: downloaded arxivv1 metadata/intro; detailed methods/results assigned to citation agent to avoid duplicate reading. Fixed-confidence ATC verifies input/BN correction in wearables, not a monitor immune to entropy updates.
- Goldwasser2020Rejectron: primary NeurIPS review and Detectron's detailed AppendixA account only; no full original paper reading. Selective prediction/PQ-learning supplies disagreement-based abstention guarantees under realizability/ERM assumptions; not used to establish a C1–C5 verdict.
- Yuan2026UnderwaterBN: primary preprints.org full text indexed §3.6,§4.3,§4.6 read as a late search lead. Confidently collapsed entropy-selected Tent checkpoints and BN-versus-affine controls are reported, but this is collapsed two-class offline SFUDA and not Schirmer. Detailed reading referred to TTA agent/parent; do not claim full-paper audit.
