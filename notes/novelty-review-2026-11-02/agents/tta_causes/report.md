# TTA, calibration and proxy-failure arm

Search and reading date: **2026-11-02 (Asia/Kolkata)**. Adversarial objective: find prior evidence against originality of C1–C5. No experiments, model inference or implementation tests were run. Python was used only for bibliographic retrieval and document processing through `uv run --no-project` with the standard library. This is one search arm, not a complete citation census.

## Most consequential findings

1. **AETTA already shows the core descriptive failure.** In CVPR2024 §3.2, Fig.2 (printed p.28645), Tent on continually changing CIFAR100-C becomes more confident as accuracy collapses, and predictions concentrate in a few classes. This breaks their original disagreement/error relation. §3.3 develops a correction using batch prediction entropy; Fig.4 and Tables1–2 compare estimated and actual batch accuracy. Supplement C, D.1 and D.4 disclose estimation limitations and a sequential heuristic recovery rule. Figure2 was visually inspected, and main methods/results plus relevant supplement were read. No Schirmer confidence-sequence alarm, true class-prior Dirichlet experiment or adapted-vs-frozen missed-risk comparison was identified in this protocol.

2. **Cygert's published 2026 paper already studies corruption plus Dirichlet proxy failure.** `Cygert2026RealisticSelection`, §5.2 and Fig.6, printed p.81, evaluate CIFAR100-C with temporal class correlation, δ∈{10,1,0.1,0.01} plus uniform. The panels cover SAR, EATA and AdaContrast. Unsupervised selection can diverge from oracle selection as correlation increases. Fig.7–8, p.82, show objective-linked entropy initially selecting good models and later choosing degenerate models. §3.1 warns that confidence need not imply accuracy. Published PDF methods, setup, results/discussion and these figures were inspected; Fig.6 was visually checked. This materially anticipates a broad claim about adaptation corrupting the signal used to judge it, including a closely related shift construction. It does not report Schirmer's alarm-power gap.

3. **A recent audit makes the dashboard failure explicit.** Wali Ahmed, Md Azizul Rahaman, Pappu Roy and Parves Alam, *The dashboard says “perfect:”...*, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, published **8 October 2026**, DOI10.3389/frai.2026.1928858. Sections3.3–3.4 define BN/entropy-based adaptation and source-calibrated ATC; §4.3, Props1–2 and Table6 show confidence/ensemble-agreement estimators endorsing degenerate classifiers; §4.6/Table8 propose conformal abstention as a drift signal. Read the full methods, result tables, propositions, mechanism discussion and limitations in publisher HTML/PDF. The experiments are a natural network-intrusion year shift with simultaneous changes in input, class mixture and class conditionals, not noncollapsed CIFAR100-C Dirichlet skew. The most dramatic failed model is a collapsed rank-representation model, so it does **not** isolate entropy minimization as its cause. No Schirmer citation or sequential risk confidence sequence is present. Its propositions are simple vulnerabilities, not universal impossibility theorems or guarantees for the proposed fix.

4. **Open-set TTA has already measured adaptation harming its own confidence filter.** Zefeng Li and Evan Shelhamer, TMLR05/2026, arXiv2606.01973. Sections3.1–3.3 define confidence-thresholded OOD rejection; Tables1/10 compare Source and Tent with newer methods. §4.6, printed p.10, measures loss changes during OOD-only intervals and concludes updates reinforce confidence on incorrect OOD predictions. Sections4.1–4.9, Tables1/10/11/13, relevant appendix and conclusion were selectively read. This is a close analogue of C3/C4, with an explicit rejection task and frozen comparator. It concerns unknown classes, not closed-set label-prior skew or sequential risk alarms.

5. **Over-certainty and attempts to repair it are established.** Amin/Kim arXiv2404.16168v4 (TMLR07/2025), §§3–4, Tables1–3/7–11 and discussion compare entropy, accuracy and calibration with no-adaptation controls. Nam et al.'s SICL (WACV2026; preprint2512.07390), §4.2/Fig.2, directly contrasts EM updates with no updates; §6.3 introduces a correction for style-invariant collapsed predictions. Their Dirichlet stream correlates **corruption types**, not class priors (§7.1; preprint AppendixC.3). Calibration improvement is not a sequential detection-power guarantee.

## Reviewer implications

- Known over-certainty is a serious objection to C3 as a contribution. A defensible C1 report must explain what the risk monitor's recalibration/one-sided bound was supposed to handle and what observable proxy property loses power beyond a shared score rescaling.
- The shift ingredients are established: NOTE/LAME, SAR, ROID, Cygert and prior-aware work already use correlated/imbalanced/corrupted data. Novelty cannot rest on Dirichlet0.1 or combining corruption and skew.
- Newer methods offer accuracy/calibration/detection repairs. COME and DSBR are necessary controls before presenting broad failure of TTA reliability. None of the inspected papers proves that they repair Schirmer's sequential alarm in the exact setting.
- A label-free fix should be compared with AETTA recovery, SICL content/style probes and source/live disagreement, and report sensitivity at a matched operational false-alarm budget. Merely replacing confidence by another adapted-model signal does not establish independence.

Recommendation from this arm: retain only a narrow empirical diagnostic claim pending the authors'-pipeline and C2 records. Stop pitching C3/C4 as new principles. The current result can motivate a diagnostic study, but broad novelty framing is not defensible; exact C1 source coverage and a useful controlled remedy remain incomplete.
