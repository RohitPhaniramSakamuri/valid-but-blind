## Inclusion and exclusion

Included: primary methods needed to assess the abstract's novelty; strong representative adaptation baselines; direct selection/monitoring/control predecessors; benchmark definitions and evaluation rules; theory that invalidates assumptions; adjacent feedback/control work that defeats a broad claim.

Excluded as scientific evidence: generated reviews and search aggregators used only to discover papers; unverified titles/identifiers; claimed numerical improvements without a traceable primary source; unrelated robotic/medical/LLM work unless it bears on a general claim being considered. Included adjacent tasks remain explicitly qualified and are not treated as matched classification baselines.

Not inferred: absent exact query hits mean no predecessor exists; a paper's future-work sentence proves a verified gap; a publisher abstract supports full-method reading; author-reported acceptance is independently confirmed; publicly linked code was executed; several nominally different benchmarks supply independent underlying images.
