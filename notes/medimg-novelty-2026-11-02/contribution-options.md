# Contribution options and a bounded medical pilot

**Recommendation: do d first.** Establish whether the controlled medical blind spot and the twin’s benefit exist before building another signal. Add b only if it detects failures missed by the twin at a comparable false-alarm rate. The literature objections are strong enough that medical terminology, an extra feature, or a sequential wrapper alone would be a weak contribution. This is a proposal; no experiment, download, installation or GPU run was performed or authorized by this review.

The rankings below concern potential contribution strength **if the specified result succeeds**, and feasibility from an existing working implementation. They are judgments, not measured runtimes.

| Option | Contribution strength rank | Three-hour feasibility rank | Defensible scope |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **a. Describe hidden errors by image properties** | 4 | 1 | A controlled explanatory analysis accompanying a stronger result; weak as a standalone method. |
| **b. Add image quality to a sequential alarm** | 1 | 3 | A useful empirical improvement only if quality contributes beyond the twin and existing correctness signals on held-out shift families. |
| **c. Add restoration consistency to an alarm** | 3 | 4 | A cost-effective residual improvement over perturbation-consistency predecessors; a filter substitution alone is insufficient. |
| **d. Test the blind spot and twin on medical data** | 2 | 2 | A bounded medical replication and stress study, with explicit limits on natural shifts and clinical interpretation. |

## a. Describe hidden errors by classical image properties

**Closest work:** [TCSR-Monitor](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16748v1), §3/Table 1 and §4.3, already uses sharpness, contrast, brightness and specularity to predict surgical segmentation failures; [MedMNIST-C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.17536), §§2–3, systematically varies medical image degradations. [Saeed et al.](https://beta.melba-journal.org/papers/2022:004.html), §1.3/Fig. 1 and §4.2, distinguish visible quality from task amenability. None establishes the specific conditional structure of Tent’s **unflagged** errors.

**What would make it strong:** a prespecified panel—Laplacian sharpness, robust noise estimate, local/global contrast, high-frequency energy and block-boundary artifacts—explains additional hidden-error risk for Tent relative to source and BN-only. The relationship should survive within-class, within-severity and within-time/state comparisons, then transfer to unseen corruption families. Separate the joint rate `P(error AND unflagged)` from `P(unflagged | error)`, which can change merely because the error pool changes. Use paired images/states and report effect sizes with uncertainty. A useful negative result would show that apparent quality effects disappear after these controls.

**What would make it worthless:** ranking features that identify the injected corruption; pooling classes until contrast becomes a class label; reporting only error-versus-severity plots; selecting properties after looking at held-out errors; or calling a fitted association the cause of adaptation failure. Noise and frequency estimates can respond to lesion texture, resizing and preprocessing. Raw-image scores must be recorded before any model-specific normalization.

## b. Add image quality to a sequential alarm

**Closest work:** TCSR already combines quality and uncertainty in a medical failure alarm (§§3–4); [MMC+](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13174v2), §§2.1/2.4, combines independent image appearance with outputs for medical drift monitoring. [Schirmer et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/746960ad49ddb47248970a0e1404230c-Paper-Conference.pdf), §§3.4/5.4, already replace a poor TTA risk proxy. Their combination is the principal anticipation objection. MMC+ measures drift rather than an anytime risk bound, and TCSR learns from labeled failures of a fixed segmenter.

**What would make it strong:** a fixed, cheap quality score or a simple development-trained combination improves detection of **noncollapsed adaptation harm** on held-out artifact families. It must beat twin-only, quality-only, frozen-reference-only, entropy-only, a TCSR-style quality/confidence predictor and an existing correctness proxy at comparable false alarms and total compute. Show the added value when a reference remains safe, and failures of the added value when adaptation successfully repairs degraded images. Report training labels and stored statistics openly; “label-free” should mean no target labels at deployment.

For a later adequately powered study, a useful prespecified target could be a reduction of at least 10 percentage points in missed clear violations **beyond twin-only**, with no increase above an agreed false-alarm budget. This is a proposed practical threshold, not a literature-backed guarantee or a claim that a three-hour pilot can estimate it precisely.

**What would make it worthless:** an OR rule that catches more failures only by alarming more often; improvements confined to corruptions used to tune the quality score; measuring perceptual degradation rather than the adapted model’s errors; or declaring a certified risk bound without establishing its proxy-to-error condition. A fixed `q(x)` cannot be inflated by changing classifier weights, but its relationship with live-model error can still fail completely. BRISQUE and NIQE do not solve that problem by themselves.

## c. Add restoration or perturbation consistency

**Closest work:** [SICL](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Nam_Towards_Reliable_Test-Time_Adaptation_Style_Invariance_as_a_Correctness_Likelihood_WACV_2026_paper.pdf), §§6–7, already derives correctness during adaptation from perturbation consistency and addresses collapsed agreement. [Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.07356), §3/§4, use medical augmentation uncertainty; [IRTTA](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.05041), §2.4/§3.5, uses medical reconstruction trajectories and evaluates uncertainty. This is a crowded mechanism.

**What would make it strong:** two or three inexpensive, medically defensible classical transforms detect a residual failure set missed by the twin, SICL and ordinary augmentation disagreement, on held-out artifact families at an equal forward-pass and false-alarm budget. Demonstrate that the improvement comes from prediction stability rather than a second adaptation trajectory. Include controls for clean images and successful adaptation; a lesion must remain meaningful after transformation.

**What would make it worthless:** renaming test-time augmentation, comparing a many-pass restoration ensemble with one entropy pass, changing BN buffers while probing, using an expensive learned reconstruction without charging its cost, or celebrating agreement after collapse. A denoiser may remove diagnostic texture and sharpening may introduce it. Source or target labels used to choose a transformation must be confined to development.

## d. Test the medical blind spot and twin

**Closest work:** [ECFR](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/0402_paper.pdf), §3.2/Table 1, already establishes an adverse medical Tent calibration comparison. [DSBR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02339), §5, studies medical entropy-adaptation failure. [Flühmann et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.22776v1), §4.2/§5.3, show that clinical metric estimation struggles with prevalence shift, but use static predictors and separate artifact/prior experiments. [ReDi-FM](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/140), §§3.2–3.3, uses frozen references and disagreement to regulate medical adaptation. The blocked AdaSwitch3D, Mahdiani, ODFD and TRUST-ADAPT sources prevent a broad priority assertion.

**What would make it strong:** a paired medical comparison shows the released monitor loses sensitivity under Tent at comparable error, attributes the difference to the entropy update beyond BN, and shows the twin recovers useful alarm power on held-out shifts with a meaningful safe-stream denominator. Include a stable TTA comparator such as SAR: the local CIFAR record does not support a universal entropy-adaptation claim. The most valuable subset is adaptation-caused harm while the frozen reference remains safe, including failures short of complete collapse. A negative replication is informative if source accuracy, shift strength and monitor headroom were adequate.

**What would make it worthless:** only ECE or confidence plots; a deliberately catastrophic learning rate; only extreme blur; a twin that simply reproduces unsafe-reference alarms; or claims about clinical scanner/prevalence drift from synthetic JPEG and class resampling. A dataset substitution with no mechanism controls has little weight. Three hours can establish a pilot, not clinical monitoring reliability.

## Dataset choice without registration delays

**First choice: DermaMNIST at 224 pixels**, using the openly downloadable [official MedMNIST+ record](https://zenodo.org/records/10519652). Its file listing gives about **1.1 GB** for `dermamnist_224.npz`; the 64-pixel version is about **100 MB** and is a fallback if time is tight. It supports lesion-class mixture stressors, although changing lesion labels synthetically is not a natural disease-prevalence study. The official [MedMNIST site](https://medmnist.com/) explicitly limits the collection to research/education rather than clinical use. The record marks DermaMNIST CC BY-NC; respect that scope. No account gate was encountered in the public file listing; transfer speed and integrity were not tested.

Before treating the result as a medical cohort result, establish lesion/patient grouping from the originating metadata and prevent group overlap between source, calibration, development and test. If that cannot be established within the pilot, report an image-level standardized-dataset stress test and retain the leakage limitation. Do not imply an audited patient-disjoint study.

| Alternative | Reason to consider it | Three-hour limitation |
|---|---|---|
| PathMNIST-64 | Public official file, about 1.1 GB; more tissue-class samples for conditional analysis. | Tissue-class proportions are not disease prevalence. At 64 pixels classical quality measures lose detail; the 224-pixel file is about 12.6 GB. |
| OCTMNIST / OCTMNIST-C | Ophthalmic modality and acquisition-quality relevance. | The clean 224-pixel file is about 4 GB; the precomputed corrupted collection is about 721 MB. Test-set size limits safe/unsafe estimates, and synthetic transforms do not recreate raw-device reconstruction changes. |
| PneumoniaMNIST-224 | Small public file, about 214 MB, and direct chest classification. | A small binary test set gives weak precision for class-skew and false-alarm claims. A useful fallback, not the preferred confirmatory dataset. |

Sizes come from the [clean-data record](https://zenodo.org/records/10519652) and [MedMNIST-C record](https://zenodo.org/records/11471504), checked on the review date. The full precomputed MedMNIST-C collection is about 184 GB, and DermaMNIST-C alone about 13.2 GB. Avoid downloading the whole collection for this pilot. Generate only selected corruptions with a pinned official implementation if its dependencies already work. The [official corruption API](https://github.com/francescodisalvo05/medmnistc-api) depends on Wand/ImageMagick; that setup may consume the time budget. A replacement implementation must be labeled as a port, not silently presented as the exact benchmark.

Camelyon17, CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR, ACDC and M&Ms remain relevant follow-up settings. Their access/readiness and full data transfer were not verified for a new three-hour session, so this plan does not promise immediate use of those datasets. Neither DermaMNIST nor resampled JPEG images establish CT low-dose or MRI scanner robustness.

## Proposed three-hour scope on one existing RTX 4090 or H100

**Resource envelope:** one already available GPU, local CPU/storage, zero paid services, a hard 180-minute wall-clock limit. This assumes a working PyTorch/uv environment, the project’s monitor implementation and an existing accessible ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-18 checkpoint. GPU availability and elapsed costs have not been benchmarked. Training from scratch, debugging a new segmentation pipeline, reproducing a diffusion restorer or obtaining restricted datasets is outside this envelope. Execution requires a later instruction; this review does not start it.

| Wall-clock allocation | Planned work and stopping condition |
|---|---|
| 0–20 min | Retrieve one clean dataset; verify checksum, splits, grouping metadata and dependency readiness. If access or setup fails, report infeasibility rather than spend or silently change the dataset. |
| 20–60 min | Fit one source ResNet-18. Reserve disjoint source calibration and shift-development images before training. Select source checkpoint using development/validation only; require useful source accuracy. |
| 60–80 min | On development families only, choose moderate adaptation settings and freeze monitor/twin rules. Fix class-mixture generation, horizons, corruption severities, tolerances and all optional quality coefficients. |
| 80–145 min | Evaluate source, BN-only, Tent and one stable TTA control on fixed held-out streams. Run the released monitor and the frozen twin rule; optionally record cheap image features. Drop optional restoration work first if the budget is tight. |
| 145–180 min | Use sealed evaluation labels to compute misses, false alarms, delay and mechanism controls. Report denominators, effective unique cases, timing and uncertainty. Stop at 180 minutes even if the result is inconclusive. |

These are allocation targets, not performance estimates. Re-evaluating a labeled source calibration buffer after every update can dominate the GPU budget; charge those forwards explicitly. Record all extra twin, prototype, teacher, restoration, feature-extraction and candidate-selection costs. SICL’s published multi-probe score is not free. A smaller probe count is a declared ablation, not a faithful full reproduction.

**Minimal design to freeze before execution:**

1. **Separation:** train/source calibration/shift development/evaluation must have disjoint images and, where available, lesion/patient groups. For example, reserve source calibration and development subsets from official training data, use the official validation set for source checkpoint selection, and seal the official test set. Exact counts depend on class/group availability; record them before accessing test outcomes. Keep development artifact families (for example noise and brightness) distinct from held-out families (for example blur and compression), not merely different severities of the same transform.
2. **Factorial shifts:** include clean/natural mixture, prevalence-only, artifact-only and joint artifact-plus-prevalence streams. Dirichlet(0.1) is a stress condition, accompanied by a milder mixture. Hidden evaluation labels may be used offline by the stream constructor and evaluator, but must never reach adaptation, routing, threshold choice or stopping. Corruption identity/severity is also unavailable to the deployed monitor.
3. **States:** start each method/stream from the same source checkpoint. Keep adaptation continual within a fixed stream; reset between streams. An episodic variant is a separate arm. A BN-only no-gradient twin is not the same function as the fully frozen source. Restoration probes, if any, use a saved identical state with weights, optimizer and normalization buffers held fixed.
4. **Controls:** retain the released monitor, a fixed-threshold version, source, BN-only, Tent, a fully frozen reference, twin-only and the twin’s documented OR/disagreement rule. A SAR comparison tests whether the observation extends beyond Tent. If adding b, include quality-only and quality-plus-twin with fixed coefficients; do not choose the best on evaluation labels. The three-hour pilot may omit expensive SICL/prototype comparisons, but then cannot support a strong method claim for b or c.
5. **Outcomes:** report each model’s clear risk violations and the shared violation subset; provide alarm misses, safe-stream false alarms and time to alarm. Separately report class-specific sensitivity and a noncollapsed adaptation-harm subset where source remains safe. Confidence/ECE and drift are secondary diagnostics. Check Schirmer’s proxy condition diagnostically with evaluator labels and distinguish a valid-but-low-power test from a violated assumption. A heuristic twin’s empirical success is not a certified bound.
6. **Precision and decision:** aim for at least 40 safe streams and 20 clear violations, with a meaningful noncollapsed subset; these are screening minima, not adequate clinical precision. Resampling a small test set creates dependence: disclose repeated images, cluster uncertainty by underlying case where possible, and do not count streams as independent patients. If adequate cases or headroom are absent, label the pilot **inconclusive**, rather than relax risk tolerances or tune on test labels. An empirical false-alarm rate below 10% on 40 streams still has wide uncertainty and does not establish a 10% guarantee.

For d, a prespecified target such as a 20-percentage-point miss-rate reduction by the twin at an agreed empirical false-alarm budget would justify a larger study, provided the effect includes noncollapsed harm and survives the controls. The thresholds are proposed decision rules, not completed results. If d fails under competent source and adaptation settings, or b contributes nothing beyond the twin, stop the corresponding contribution claim. Do not build a learned controller merely to rescue it.
