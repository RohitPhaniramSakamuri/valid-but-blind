## Evidence per claim

### M1: image properties of hidden errors

**Paper findings.** TCSR’s §3/Table 1 uses Laplacian variance, grayscale contrast and brightness, plus specularity; §4.3 tests whether failure prediction merely recognizes corruption. Its segmenter is fixed. MedMNIST-C §2/Table 1 and §3 study modality-specific degradations; DSBR §5/Figs. 1–4 studies entropy-adaptation failure on medical domains. These establish ingredient overlap, without the proposed conditional hidden-error analysis. [TCSR full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16748v1); [MedMNIST-C full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.17536); [DSBR full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02339).

**Inference.** A graph of error versus blur or corruption severity would add little. Define hidden error as `wrong AND not flagged` under a fixed monitor specification; distinguish its joint frequency from the conditional miss rate among errors. Compare source, BN-only and Tent on the same images and externally fixed streams. Test whether a property predicts *additional* hidden errors after class, severity, error level and model-state/time controls. Do not infer “scanner shift” or its cause from a marginal image score.

### M2: immutable image quality as an alarm signal

**Paper findings.** TCSR is the closest direct collision, including confidence-only and held-out-corruption comparisons, but its “temporal” features and per-frame threshold are **not** an anytime sequential risk test. It learns from labeled segmentation failures and calibrates with labeled cases; only deployment is label-free. Its §4.4/Table 5 also reports a transfer setting where entropy is stronger. [TCSR](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16748v1).

MRIQC already provides no-reference medical quality features and include/exclude decisions. It predicts expert **image-quality ratings**, not errors of a live adapted classifier; Table 4/Fig. 7 expose limited sensitivity to an unseen artifact. [Esteban et al., PLOS ONE 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5612458/), Methods “Image quality metrics,” Results “Evaluation on held-out data.”

Saeed et al. explicitly distinguish task-agnostic quality from task amenability; §4.2/Table 1 and Fig. 7 show why visible artifacts and poor segmentation need not coincide. Task-specific and shaped variants use clinical task labels during training; the fully task-agnostic branch uses self-reconstruction without clinical or expert quality labels (§2.2). These are learned controllers rather than purely handcrafted scores. [MELBA 2022, IPMI 2021 special issue](https://beta.melba-journal.org/papers/2022:004.html).

**Inference.** The phrase “adaptation cannot inflate” is true only in a narrow computational sense: for an unchanged raw image, a fixed function `q(x)` is invariant to classifier-weight updates. It does not keep `P(error of f_t | q)` fixed. With identical inputs and a damaged classifier, `q(x)` stays unchanged while risk rises. Conversely, effective adaptation can make degraded images safe while the quality alarm keeps firing. BRISQUE is learned using perceptual quality labels; NIQE uses a fitted pristine natural-image model. Neither is an assumption-free medical error estimator. [Original BRISQUE](https://utw10503.utweb.utexas.edu/publications/2012/TIP%20BRISQUE.pdf), §III.B/§IV.A; [NIQE publisher record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/6353522/authors), abstract only in this review.

An OR-combination with the twin can increase false alarms. A new proxy must establish its own relationship to error; feeding it into Schirmer’s code does not inherit the old proxy’s validity assumption. A practical detector and a certified bound must be reported separately.

### M3: restoration and perturbation consistency

**Paper findings.** SICL is an especially close predecessor: its correctness score uses consistency under feature-style perturbations, with a content-perturbation correction for collapsed predictions; it is not a classical image-restoration method. Its dynamic Dirichlet construction mixes **corruption domains**, not class priors. [SICL](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/papers/Nam_Towards_Reliable_Test-Time_Adaptation_Style_Invariance_as_a_Correctness_Likelihood_WACV_2026_paper.pdf), §§6.1–6.3, §7.1/Table 2.

Medical perturbation uncertainty predates it: Wang et al. §3 models transformations/noise and §4 examines uncertainty versus segmentation errors. Here TTA means **test-time augmentation**, with fixed weights. IRTTA is closer to restoration plus adaptation: it adapts a modulator on a diffusion-reconstruction trajectory and evaluates medical uncertainty, without a sequential alarm. [Wang et al., Neurocomputing 2019](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.07356), §3.3/§4.1.4/Figs. 4–5; [IRTTA, MIDL 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.05041), §2.4/§3.5.

**Inference.** Replacing feature perturbations with denoising, sharpening or resampling is a small mechanism change unless it detects failures that SICL, simple augmentation disagreement and the twin miss. Restorations may remove a lesion or create edges. A collapsed model can agree on every transformed input. Measure scores at an identical saved model state without optimizer or BN-buffer mutations; otherwise the probe itself changes the counterfactual.

### M4: medical calibration damage

**Decisive published evidence.** ECFR Table 1 compares zero-shot and Tent on Ark+ → Mendeley-V2 chest radiographs and DermLIP → Daffodil/HAM dermatology. For the chest task, ECE is **0.5270 → 0.5873** and Brier score **0.4116 → 0.4954**; the Daffodil calibration difference is also adverse, while HAM improves. Fig. 3 follows erroneous predictions toward low-metric regions. Thus the existential calibration part is already published; a universal medical or entropy-method claim is false. [Li, Gu and Zhang, MICCAI 2026](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/paper/0402_paper.pdf), §3.2/Table 1/Fig. 3.

Access qualifications matter: ECFR-Acc uses a scalar target-reliability prior, instantiated using zero-shot target accuracy in its public benchmark (§3.1); its stronger results cannot be imported as a purely unlabeled baseline. DSBR Appendix G/Table 10 additionally discusses medical ROC-AUC limits, which are **not** failure-detection AUROC. [Nielen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02339), §5/Appendix G.

### M5: reference disagreement

**Paper findings.** ReDi-FM uses a frozen source model and frozen SAM to guide a continually adapting medical segmenter; its consensus gate regulates distillation, rather than raising a sequential risk-violation alarm. Zenk et al. benchmark disagreement-based medical segmentation quality signals, including failures missed when ensemble members agree on an error. [ReDi-FM, IJCAI 2026](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/140), §§3.2–3.3/Table 4; [Zenk et al., MedIA 2025](https://inrepo02.dkfz.de/record/294922/files/1-s2.0-S1361841524003177-main.pdf?version=1), §4.4.3/§5.5/Fig. 6; main-text preprint also read.

The earlier review already documents CUI’s source/current-model comparisons and Han’s frozen residual sentinel. CUI’s experiments are natural images; Han uses revealed forecasting outcomes. Neither should be relabeled a medical label-free twin alarm. [Prior extraction](../novelty-review-2026-11-02/review.md), C5 and closest-work matrix.

**Inference.** A source clone with test-batch BN statistics is no-gradient but not an immutable predictor: its output depends on batch composition. Compare it with a genuinely frozen source and BN alone. Disagreement can arise from a beneficial update; agreement can be jointly wrong. These controls determine whether the twin monitors the adapted model’s risk or mainly reproduces the reference’s failures.

### M6: joint artifact/prevalence/adaptation masking

**Paper findings.** Flühmann et al. evaluate label-free clinical metric estimation under real CXR domain shifts, synthetic artifact association, and prevalence shifts. In §4.2 the artifact experiment **holds prevalence fixed**, and the prevalence experiment is separate; §5.3/Fig. 4 reports estimation failures under prevalence shift. The predictors do not adapt online. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.22776v1), §§4–6.

Ma et al. already adapt medical classifiers for label-distribution shift, using multiple trained heads and simultaneous access to a test set; this is not a sequential-monitor experiment. DSBR’s explicit label-shift stress experiment is on **ImageNet-C**, not a medical artifact-plus-prior monitor experiment. [Ma et al., MICCAI 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.00769), §2.3/§3; [DSBR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02339), §5.2/Table 3.

**Exact absence boundary.** No inspected accessible source joins all four: a medical predictor adapting through entropy minimization; medical acquisition/artifact change jointly manipulated with prevalence; a label-free sequential risk alarm; and a quantified adapted-versus-frozen missed-failure comparison. This does not exclude joint-shift studies without these keywords, unindexed 2026 work, or blocked papers. Targeted searches covered Camelyon17, CheXpert/MIMIC-CXR, cardiac ACDC and M&Ms, MRI/CT artifacts, MedMNIST-C, monitoring and quality-control families. “ACDC” road-scene results were excluded from medical evidence. Blockers are itemized below.

## What remains unaddressed

1. **A conditional mechanism.** Whether classical properties explain Tent-specific unflagged errors beyond known class/corruption/state effects. A property’s feature importance is not a causal explanation.
2. **Residual alarm power.** Whether quality or restoration adds sensitivity beyond twin-only, frozen-only, Schirmer’s prototype proxy, SICL and ordinary augmentation disagreement, at a fixed false-alarm budget and complete compute cost.
3. **Adaptation-only medical harm.** Useful detection when the frozen reference remains safe but the live model is harmed, including noncollapsed states and clinically relevant minority-class sensitivity.
4. **Joint shift realism.** Synthetic class sampling and synthetic image corruption are controlled stressors. They do not demonstrate natural scanner change plus disease-prevalence drift or clinical readiness.
5. **Sequential validity.** Per-image AUROC, ECE, conformal coverage and “temporal” features do not imply anytime false-alarm control, nor do non-alarms certify safety.

The [contribution options](contribution-options.md) recommend **d first**, then b only if the medical blind spot leaves residual headroom. A three-hour run is a falsification pilot, not a completed medical benchmark.
